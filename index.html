Pitch & Transpose

Playback pitch offset — Not implemented yet

A playback-only offset shifts synthesized sound; it does not change written music or expected MIDI pitches. Capo/down-tuning practice needs coordinated expected-pitch handling.

Transpose score — Not implemented yet

Future transposition must keep notation, key signatures, tab positions and practice targets consistent.

Help & Guide

  1. Open a Guitar Pro or MusicXML score from Scores.
  2. Select your Fishman or other MIDI input in Settings. Choose Poly/single-channel or your configured six-channel Hex layout.
  3. In Practice, choose a track and Realtime or Wait. Both currently grade pitch.
  4. Use Tempo and Loop to work on a section. Play starts with a count-in; Pause preserves results.

Visual feedback is in Practice; track visibility and the optional six-string fretboard are in Display. Audio controls selected/backing-track playback.

Poly fret estimates are optional guidance, not detected positions. Left-handed fretboard orientation does not reverse MIDI channel mapping.
