# Guitar Trainer Studio v1.1.0

- **Practice from printed music.** Open a PDF with a local recording for play-along practice, or pair it with a playable score for Wait mode and MIDI grading. Scan measure boxes across the whole PDF, correct them, and map repeats and alternate endings.
- **Open MIDI files and see what you played.** Import a `.mid` or `.midi` file, choose the part to practice, and switch between the fretboard and an 88-key keyboard showing expected, correct, wrong, and held notes.
- **Match a score to a recording.** Auto-sync suggests measure timings for lessons and continuous performances. Audition alternate takes, skip spoken breaks, fill gaps, and refine timing between two fixed points.
- **Build speed through repetition.** Speed Trainer raises the tempo after a chosen number of Realtime synth loops. You can also choose a different instrument sound for each melodic track.
- **Reopen a practice setup.** Library entries restore companion PDFs, measure markings, repeat mappings, and saved recording timing. Reconnect a moved recording and carry PDF markings and timing to another device through a library backup.

**Validation.** The release checks passed 984 automated tests, with nine skipped. Guitar grading requires MIDI input. Listen to suggested measure starts before using them for graded practice, especially around repeated takes or spoken breaks.

**Updating.** Back up your library, then refresh the hosted trainer or download the source ZIP. Use the same browser and address to retain your library and settings.
